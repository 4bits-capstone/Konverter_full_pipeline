  - Benchmarked 31-page, 164-page, and 284-page (`VLRC_Inclusive-Juries`) PDFs in isolated offline subprocesses.
  - `generate_parsed_pages=True` adds only **~9.6 KiB/page** (`2.67 MiB` on 284 pages, `< 0.1%` of the `2.41–2.96 GiB` peak RSS), whereas setting `generate_parsed_pages=False` silently breaks `HeadingHierarchyOptions(use_style=True)` and collapses **48.9%–64.0% of section headers** to `level=1`.
- **Offline Picture Classification & Description Evaluation (R4 / Gap #10):** [r4_picture_evaluation.md](file:///Users/yethuaung/Konverter_full_pipeline/../.gemini/antigravity/brain/060da294-1184-4986-99c9-9bb99cc7cc40/r4_picture_evaluation.md)
  - Evaluated `DocumentFigureClassifier-v2.5` (`do_picture_classification`) and `SmolVLM-256M-Instruct` (`do_picture_description`) across the Capstone report and 5 VLRC reports (1,016 pages, 94 pictures).
  - `do_picture_classification` tags page numbers, heading accents, and chapter dividers as `logo`/`icon` with `0.84–0.995` confidence (indistinguishable from real logos) and forces `keep_backend=True`.
  - `do_picture_description` (`SmolVLM-256M-Instruct`) adds **74–103s per image** on CPU, skips 87% of kept VLRC figures under the 5% area threshold, leaks `<end_of_utteranc` tokens in 5/9 outputs, and hallucinates descriptions. Both remain `False` by default.
  - The evaluation also uncovered 3 non-VLM follow-up items in existing pipeline code (see Section 7 of [r4_picture_evaluation.md](file:///Users/yethuaung/.gemini/antigravity/brain/060da294-1184-4986-99c9-9bb99cc7cc40/r4_picture_evaluation.md)).
